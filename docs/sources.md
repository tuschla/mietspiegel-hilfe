Method:
- Tables: `pdftotext -layout` (poppler) of PDF 17–21, parsed row by row (`tools/extract_data.py`). Every table page of both years was rendered with `pdftoppm -r 110` and compared visually; 20 value spot checks and 12 row lookups (incl. the brochures' own worked examples) are encoded in `tools/verify_data.py` with page numbers. All 189 (2026) and 163 (2024) rows were also compared number by number with an independent text extract of the separate official sheet "Berliner Mietspiegeltabelle" 2026/2024: 0 differences.
- Features: plain `pdftotext` of PDF 24–26 (soft hyphens are removed by poppler), split at the ☐ boxes; three line-end hard hyphens that poppler drops were restored after checking the page images ("Lärm- und", "Dienstleistungs- und", "Verbrauchsenergiekennwert-Grenzen"). The energy box on PDF 26 (two columns joined by »oder«) was transcribed by hand from the same text and the image. For 2026 the column heading "(End-)Energieverbrauchskennwert" is prefixed to each of the three boxes below it; in 2024 each box carries the word itself.
- The tables contain no per-cell markers (no asterisks, no shaded "limited case number" cells) in either year, so all `flags` are empty. The only markers are * / ** on the Bezugsfertigkeit labels "1973 bis 1990 Ost*" and "1991 bis 2001**"; their footnotes are in `dims.baualter[].footnote_de` and `notes_de`.
- Feature ids are stable concepts across both years: 2026 is numbered in official order; 2024 reuses the 2026 id for the same item. Items only in 2026: `wohnung-m08` (Erdgeschoss without break-in/privacy protection). Items only in 2024: `umfeld-m04` (Lage in stark vernachlässigter Umgebung), `umfeld-p05` (Bevorzugte Citylage). 2024 "Rollläden" and 2026 "Außen angebrachte Verschattungssysteme … (z. B. Rollläden)" share `wohnung-p06`.
- Machine-readable helpers derived from the official wording (not official fields): `baualter_excluded` only where the scope note names whole Baualter classes unambiguously; `excl`/`kwh_gt`/`kwh_lt` from the energy box and Nr. 10.4 A. Where the scope is ambiguous or covers only one part of an item, there is no exclusion; instead `note_de` holds the verbatim official fragment so the UI can ask the user: bad-p08, bad-p09, umfeld-p02, gebaeude-p08 (exclusion removed after review, 2026-10-18), wohnung-m08 (2026), and the Denkmalschutz sentence on gebaeude-m08…m11.

Notable wording differences 2024 → 2026 (verbatim in the JSON files): the 2024 rule "Für dezentrale Warmwasserversorgung sind … 20 kWh/(m²a) auf den ausgewiesenen Energiekennwert aufzuschlagen" became in 2026 "Für Kennwerte ohne Berücksichtigung der dezentralen Warmwasserversorgung sind … 20 kWh/(m²a) auf den (End-)Energiekennwert aufzuschlagen"; Abschlag Minderausstattung 0,45 €/m² (bis 1964) → 0,33 €/m² (bis 1949).

- Die Briefe bestehen aus festen, vorab formulierten Textbausteinen; der Nutzer wählt Vorlage und Merkmale aus der amtlichen Liste und trägt Fakten (Name, Adresse, Daten, Beträge) ein.
