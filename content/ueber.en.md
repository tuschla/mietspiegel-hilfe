## What this site is

Free, ad-free, neutral help in plain language for tenants in Berlin, to understand the Berlin rent index (Mietspiegel) and a rent increase demand (Mieterhöhungsverlangen). Not a service of a public authority.

All calculations run only in your browser. Your entries are not sent to any server. There are no cookies, no statistics or tracking programs in your browser and no content from other websites. Your browser keeps your entries only while this tab is open, so that you can move between the calculator, the check and the letter. The button “Delete my entries” removes them at once.

Each time you open a page, your browser technically sends your IP address to the server. The server only logs the page, the time and whether an automated program opened it (such page views are not counted); it does not log an IP address, any browser data or which page you came from. It deletes these entries after 7 days. Only the daily number of page views per page and language is kept permanently. Only with certain serious errors, for example when the connection is being set up or on the server itself, can an error log contain an IP address. It is also deleted after 7 days.

## What this site is not

- Not legal advice: we do not check your case. The calculators and letters apply fixed rules and text modules to your own details. They do not say how a court would decide. Results such as “Above the cap (Kappungsgrenze)” are calculation results, not a legal assessment. You decide whether and how you use a letter.
- Not an official translation: only the official German text of the laws and of the Berlin Mietspiegel is binding. The Mietspiegel is published by the Senate Department for Urban Development, Building and Housing (Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen) at <https://mietspiegel.berlin.de>. Our explanations and all translations are unofficial.
- No substitute for advice: [Help and advice](@/hilfe.en.md).

## Sources and date

We checked the legal situation on 17 and 18 October 2026. It is based on the laws (above all the German Civil Code, Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch, BGB), the Berlin regulations, the official Mietspiegel and some court decisions that we have read in full. The sources are given in the text in each case. No lawyer has checked the texts so far. Laws and case law can change.

The Wohnlage by address in the calculator comes from the open data “Wohnlagen nach Adressen zum Berliner Mietspiegel” ([2026](https://daten.berlin.de/datensaetze/wohnlagen-nach-adressen-zum-berliner-mietspiegel-2026-wfs-809faebe), [2024](https://daten.berlin.de/datensaetze/wohnlagen-nach-adressen-zum-berliner-mietspiegel-2024-wfs-eddbff85)) of the Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen, licence [Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero 2.0](https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/zero-2-0), retrieved on 10 November 2026.
