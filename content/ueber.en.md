## What this site is

This site helps tenants in Berlin to understand the Berlin rent index (Mietspiegel) and a rent increase demand (Mieterhöhungsverlangen). It is free and has no advertising. It is not a service of a public authority.

All calculations run only in your browser. Your entries are not sent to a server. As with any website, the server receives technical data when you open a page, for example your IP address. There are no cookies, no statistics programs and no content from other websites. Your browser keeps what you enter only while this tab is open, so that you can move between the calculator, the check and the letter. The button “Delete my entries” removes it at once.

## What this site is not

- **Not legal advice.** We give general information. We do not check your case. The calculators and letters only work with your own details and with fixed rules and text modules. They do not say how a court would decide in your case. Results such as “Above the cap (Kappungsgrenze)” are calculation results based on your details, not a legal assessment.
- **Not an official translation.** Only the official German text of the Berlin Mietspiegel is binding, published by the Senate Department for Urban Development, Building and Housing (Senatsverwaltung für Stadtentwicklung, Bauen und Wohnen) at <https://mietspiegel.berlin.de>. Our explanations and all translations are unofficial.
- **Not a replacement for advice.** Before you agree to a rent increase or send a letter, it is best to get free advice: [Help and advice](@/hilfe.en.md).

## The letters

The letters are templates with fixed text modules. You choose a template and enter your details: names, addresses, dates, amounts and – if you want – features from the Mietspiegel. This produces a letter in German. We do not check whether your details are correct or whether the letter fits your case. You decide yourself whether and how you use it. Read the letter carefully before you send it.

## Sources and date

We checked the legal situation on this site on 17 and 18 October 2026. We rely on the laws (above all the German Civil Code, Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch, BGB), the Berlin regulations, the official Mietspiegel and some court decisions that we have read in full. No lawyer has checked the texts so far. Laws and case law can change. The sources are given in the text in each case.

## Languages

The site is available in these languages: Deutsch (plain German), English, Türkçe, العربية, Українська, Русский and Polski. All translations are unofficial. Only the official German text of the Mietspiegel and of the laws is binding. Letters to the landlord are always in German.
