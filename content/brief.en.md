**Only for a rent increase you are asked to agree to (§ 558 BGB).** For modernisation, new advance payments for service charges (Betriebskosten), index or graduated rent (Index- oder Staffelmiete) and “cost rent” (Kostenmiete), you usually owe the higher rent even without your consent; for a furnished room at the landlord’s home, a flat let for temporary use or a student or youth hall of residence, the rules on rent increases do not apply ([more](@/mieterhoehung.en.md)). Do not simply stop paying then.

- Get free [advice](@/hilfe.en.md) before you send the letter. What your reply does: [Agree, agree in part, refuse](@/mieterhoehung.en.md).
- Read the letter carefully and do not write anything in it that is not true.
- If several people sign, change „ich“ (I) to „wir“ (we).
