The Berlin rent index (Mietspiegel) shows the local comparative rent (ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete): the usual net cold rent (Nettokaltmiete, without service charges and heating costs) per m² per month for similar flats.

Each field has a lower value, an average value and an upper value. Where your flat lies in this range is shown by the Orientierungshilfe (orientation guide) with five groups: bathroom, kitchen, flat, building and surroundings. If a group has more plus than minus features, it counts as plus; if it has more minus features, as minus; if they are equal, it does not count. One plus group and one minus group cancel each other out. Each remaining plus group moves the value up by one fifth of the distance from the average value to the upper value, each remaining minus group moves it down by one fifth of the distance from the average value to the lower value. Example: 3 plus groups and 2 minus groups result in 1 plus group. This is not 20 % more rent.
