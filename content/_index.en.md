The check and the letter only apply if your landlord asks you to agree to a higher rent (consent, Zustimmung; § 558 BGB). For modernisation, service charges (Betriebskosten), index or graduated rent (Index- oder Staffelmiete) and “cost rent” (Kostenmiete), [other rules](@/mieterhoehung.en.md) apply. **Do not simply stop paying then**; get [free advice](@/hilfe.en.md) instead.
