Here you can find the features of the Orientierungshilfe (orientation guide) for the Berlin rent index (Mietspiegel) 2024. You need it mainly in two cases:

- You received a rent increase demand (Mieterhöhungsverlangen) between 30.05.2024 and 27.05.2026.
- You are checking the rent brake (Mietpreisbremse) and your tenancy agreement started during this time.

Most features are the same as in the Mietspiegel 2026. Some texts are slightly different. We point this out for the features concerned. Only the official German text is binding.
