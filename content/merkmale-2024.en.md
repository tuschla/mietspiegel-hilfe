You need this Mietspiegel mainly for a rent increase demand (Mieterhöhungsverlangen) that arrived from 30.05.2024 to 27.05.2026, and for the rent brake (Mietpreisbremse) if your tenancy started during this time. More: [Which Mietspiegel applies?](@/mieterhoehung.en.md) and [Rent brake](@/mietpreisbremse.en.md).

- Most features are the same as in 2026. Where the text differs, there is a note at the feature.
- Only the fittings the landlord provided count, not things you installed yourself without being paid back the costs.
