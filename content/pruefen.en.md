**Does this apply to you?** This page only applies if your landlord asks you to agree to a higher rent (consent, Zustimmung; rent increase under § 558 BGB, Mieterhöhung). It does not apply to an increase after modernisation, to new advance payments for service charges (Betriebskosten), to index rent or graduated rent (Index- oder Staffelmiete), or in old social housing with “cost rent” („Kostenmiete“). In those cases you usually have to pay the higher rent even without consent. It also does not apply to a furnished room in the landlord’s flat, a flat let only for temporary use, or a student or youth hall of residence (Studenten- oder Jugendwohnheim). There the rules on rent increases do not apply.

In these cases, do **not** simply stop paying. Get advice: [Help and advice](@/hilfe.en.md). More on this: [Does this page apply to your letter?](@/mieterhoehung.en.md)

Here you check a rent increase demand (Mieterhöhungsverlangen) under § 558 BGB. This is a letter in which the landlord asks you to agree to a higher rent because your rent is below the local comparative rent (ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete).

For this you need:

- the landlord’s letter,
- the date it reached you,
- your current net cold rent (Nettokaltmiete: rent without service charges and heating costs),
- the day since which this rent has applied,
- the rent from three years ago.

If you have already used the calculator, you can copy the comparative rent from there with one click (button next to the field “Local comparative rent (ortsübliche Vergleichsmiete) in € per m²”).

The result is a calculation aid based on fixed rules and your own details. It is not a legal check of your case and does not replace advice. For your own deadlines, the calculation shows calendar dates. It is not certain whether your deadline moves if it ends on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday. It is better to reply a few days earlier. For the landlord’s deadline to sue, we show the next working day, because he then has until that day (§ 193 BGB).

Read more under [Rent increase: the main rules](@/mieterhoehung.en.md).
