As of 17.10.2026. Times and places can change. Check them on the linked pages.

## Free of charge

### Tenant advice in your district (Mieterberatung)

Every Berlin district (Bezirk) has open tenant advice services, free of charge for all tenants of the district. They do not represent you in court. List of districts with links at the Senate:
<https://www.berlin.de/sen/wohnen/service/serviceadressen/mieterberatungen-in-den-bezirken/>

### The Senate’s rent service telephone (Servicetelefon Miete)

(030) 90173 3860 for questions about the Berlin Mietspiegel and about rent. Questions on tenancy law are answered by the advice services in the districts.

### Mietpreisprüfstelle Berlin (rent check office)

Free first consultation by the State of Berlin for all tenant households in Berlin if your rent may be too high, for example because of the rent brake (Mietpreisbremse). By appointment only. Information also in English.
<https://www.berlin.de/mietpreispruefstelle>

### Advice in several languages

At the Welcome Centre (Willkommenszentrum) of the Senate’s Commissioner for Integration, you can search for advice centres by the topic “Housing” and by your language:
<https://willkommenszentrum.berlin.de/en/counseling-centers>

## For tenants of the state-owned housing companies

The ombudsman office for tenants of the state-owned housing companies (Ombudsstelle für Mieterinnen und Mieter der landeseigenen Wohnungsunternehmen) is responsible for tenants of degewo, GESOBAU, Gewobag, HOWOGE, STADT UND LAND, WBM and their subsidiaries. For berlinovo, it is only responsible for the “classic” rental flats that berlinovo owns itself, not for flats belonging to its fund companies. It gives advice in German and English on the cooperation agreement (Kooperationsvereinbarung) and the affordability promise (Leistbarkeitsversprechen), not on general questions of tenancy law.
<https://www.ombud-lwu.de/>

## Tenants’ associations (with a membership fee)

Tenants’ associations (Mietervereine) give advice to their members. Many also offer insurance for the costs of a court case. Before joining, ask about the fee, conditions, waiting times and the minimum membership period.

- Berliner Mieterverein: <https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de>
- Berliner MieterGemeinschaft: <https://www.bmgev.de>
- Mietervereinigung Berlin: <https://www.mietervereinigung-berlin.de>
- Mieterschutzbund Berlin: <https://www.mieterschutzbund-berlin.de>
- AMV (Alternativer Mieter- und Verbraucherschutzbund): <https://mieter-verbraucherschutz.berlin>

## Lawyers

For a court case or a check of your individual case, there are lawyers for tenancy law (Rechtsanwältinnen and Rechtsanwälte für Mietrecht). Without legal expenses insurance (Rechtsschutzversicherung), this costs money. If you have little money, you can apply to the local court (Amtsgericht) for advice assistance (Beratungshilfe) to pay for advice (Beratungshilfegesetz). For a court case there is legal aid (Prozesskostenhilfe, § 114 ZPO). If you lose the case, you still have to pay the landlord’s costs (§ 123 ZPO).

According to its own information, the Verbraucherzentrale Berlin (consumer advice centre) does not give advice on tenancy law.
